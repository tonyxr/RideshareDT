40% own profit + 60% own-minus-rival profit, with its existing normalization.

daily online earnings. On a day without online exposure, potential entrants
respond to the posted earning opportunity; zero pay with positive exposure
remains genuine zero income. Fleet realizations use unbiased stochastic
rounding, not a minimum of one. This is a changed supply mechanism requiring
new calibration, not an empirically validated labour-supply specification.

15 burn-in days. Revision-5 operating points are incompatible. Explicit CLI
conflicts fail before rollouts; effective parameters are recorded.

uses the same full-day statistic as its target. MPC compares with hold and
includes move cost once, without an additional fixed improvement threshold.

are relative and unsaturated. Epoch-wide PPO KL stopping belongs only to
corrected learner revision >=4, never flags-off or earlier learners.
